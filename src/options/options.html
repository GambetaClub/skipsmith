What should be skipped

Each SponsorBlock category can auto-skip, wait for a button, sit on the seek bar, or stay hidden. Sponsor, self promotion, and interaction reminders start on auto skip.

Shortcut

Alt+Shift+S skips whatever segment is under the playhead, including ones that only show a button or a seek-bar mark.

Change it on chrome://extensions/shortcuts or, in Brave, brave://extensions/shortcuts.

Privacy

        

          Skipsmith asks SponsorBlock with the first four characters of the SHA-256 of the video id. The request does not include the id.
          Skipping a segment is not reported. Time saved stays in this browser. There is no analytics.
